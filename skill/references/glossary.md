# Skill workflow glossary

This file is the canonical glossary for the installed skill. It distinguishes source description, source-preserving organization, and proposals beyond the source.

## Source Fact
A **Source Fact** is an action, condition, constraint, resource, or relationship explicitly stated by the source. A related quotation does not prove every claim about that topic.

## Source-preserving Abstraction
A **Source-preserving Abstraction** groups or splits source material while preserving its conditions, order, optionality, and scope. It adds no behavior or relationship absent from the source. Every abstraction carries a rationale and exact evidence.

## Engineering Recommendation
An **Engineering Recommendation** is an action, gate, or relationship proposed from engineering judgment that the source does not specify. It is outside the source-backed graph and requires an explicit improvement request.

## Semantic Stage
A **Semantic Stage** is a continuous action unit organized around a clear behavioral objective. Its boundary follows behavior and conditions, not document headings or numbering. A stage has an independently inspectable completion criterion.

## Coverage gap
A **coverage gap** records an outcome, destination, or continuation that the source leaves unspecified. It remains visible as a warning or coverage entry; it is not completed by an invented route.
